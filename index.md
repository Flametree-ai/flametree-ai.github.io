# Flametree

## AI Customer Operations Platform

Controlled AI customer interactions with full customer context and complete visibility.

Flametree helps banks and fintechs deploy omnichannel AI agents across customer operations, combining customer context, controlled agent behavior, AI-human collaboration, outbound engagement, and end-to-end visibility.

## Use Cases

### Acquisition & Conversion
Convert visitors into qualified leads through 24/7 AI-driven engagement.

### Onboarding & Activation
Recover abandoned onboarding journeys by identifying drop-off reasons and guiding users to completion.

### Service Operations
Resolve complex customer requests in real time, execute authorized operations through connected systems, escalate when needed, and trigger relevant next-best actions.

### Collections Management
Automate collections through timely, two-way engagement to improve repayment rates and reduce operational effort.

## Platform

- Customer context and a 360-degree view built from connected data and linked customer identities
- Seamless AI-human handoff and Copilot capabilities
- AI-powered outbound campaigns and analytics across customer journeys
- Model-agnostic architecture
- On-premises, private cloud, and cloud deployment
- Integrations through APIs and standard protocols
- Multichannel and multilingual communication
